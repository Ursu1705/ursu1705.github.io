About Endeavor

Endeavor is the Global Network of Trust of, by and for entrepreneurs. The organization selects, supports and invests in founders with the greatest potential, providing them with a platform to train, mentor, and invest in the next generation through the Endeavor Multiplier Effect™.

The Challenge

Endeavor's ecosystem had grown organically over time, creating fragmented narratives across initiatives — making it difficult for users to understand what Endeavor is, how its programs connect, and what the Multiplier Effect actually represents.

The Challenge was conceptual

The problem wasn't just navigational. Users approached the platform through goals and intent, while programs were organized by internal operational structure. A fundamental mismatch.

My contribution

UX Strategy and Information Architecture to redefine the ecosystem narrative, plus Design System contribution to ensure the new interface patterns were scalable and consistent.

Discovery

Discovery & Strategic Alignment

Since the project focused on ecosystem positioning and communication clarity, the discovery phase centered on internal stakeholders and marketing insights rather than traditional end-user research. The goal was to align perspectives across teams, identify conceptual gaps, and define a coherent ecosystem narrative.

Stakeholder Interviews

Understanding how different teams defined Endeavor, its initiatives, and the intended ecosystem relationships.

Organizational Mapping

Analyzing how programs, initiatives, and platform experiences were currently structured and communicated across teams.

Marketing Insights

Synthesizing recurring feedback regarding ecosystem comprehension, terminology confusion, and navigation expectations.

Audience Segmentation

One of the key outputs of discovery was defining who the platform actually serves — four distinct segments with very different needs and access levels.

Public — general visitors and potential entrepreneurs
Researchers — academics and analysts using Endeavor data
Institutions — partner organizations and investors
Network Members — selected Endeavor founders and mentors
Insights

Key Insights

Fragmented perception

Different stakeholders described Endeavor and the Multiplier Effect inconsistently, leading to fragmented communication patterns across the platform.

Conceptual ambiguity

Users often failed to understand the relationship between Endeavor and the Multiplier Effect ecosystem — a conceptual, not just navigational, challenge.

Internal logic ≠ user logic

Programs were organized by operational structure, while users approached the platform through goals, opportunities, and intent.

Strategy

Design Strategy

Clarify Multiplier Effect narrative entry flow

Problem

Concept explanation, ecosystem value, and interactive maps competed for attention — making it difficult to understand what the Multiplier Effect actually is.

Solution

Defined a research-led narrative hierarchy (concept → ecosystem → impact), based on stakeholder interviews, to guide users progressively through the story.

Impact

Improved comprehension, reduced cognitive overload, and created a clearer, more engaging entry point to the platform.

Information Architecture redesign

Problem

Login, maps, and participation paths were mixed, creating confusion between public exploration and restricted network access.

Solution

Separated the IA into two clear paths: Education (newsletter-driven) for public audiences, and Restricted access (login + interactive maps) for network members.

Impact

Clarified user journeys, reduced friction, and established a scalable structure that aligns content, access, and user expectations.

Design System

Design System Contribution

First State

Endeavor had an existing Design System with foundational components: form components, colors, typography styles, and CTAs.

The Gap

The redesign introduced a modern interface featuring interactive media, video content, and smooth scroll transitions — patterns not covered by the existing system.

Restrictions

The solution needed to align with the existing Design System, ensuring scalability, avoiding component duplication, and preserving cross-platform consistency.

Component Audit Process

1

Inventory of the Existing System

Mapped all existing components: names, variants, and current use cases. What already exists? What does each component do?

2

Identify New Product Needs

Based on the new user flows and screens, which UI patterns are repeated? What's new vs. what can be extended?

3

Decision: Extend vs. Create

Can this new pattern be solved using a variant of an existing component, or does it require a brand new one?

4

Add to the System with Documentation

Defined the component name, variants, usage guidelines, and added them to the shared design system.

New components added

Website Navbar

Responsive navigation with dropdown menus and authentication states for public and network-member contexts.

Founder Cards

Card component for founders carousel, with photo, name, country, and impact metrics. Built for the Multiplier Effect storytelling section.

Video Carousel

Video content component with autoplay, caption overlay, and carousel navigation — new interaction pattern not previously in the system.

Final Experience

Final design

The final platform covers five key sections — Home, Insights, About Us, Newsletter, and Multiplier Effect — each with a clear narrative role and audience alignment defined during the IA redesign phase.
