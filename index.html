How I work

My design process

Every project is different, but my approach is always human-centered and evidence-based.

01
Empathize
Contextual interviews, ethnographic observation, stakeholder alignment. I start by truly understanding people — not just what they say, but what they mean.
02
Define
Affinity mapping, personas, journey maps. I synthesize research into clear problem statements and design opportunities backed by evidence.
03
Ideate
Co-creation workshops, crazy 8s, competitive benchmarking. I explore a wide solution space before converging on the most promising directions.
04
Design
Wireframes, flows, high-fidelity prototypes. I design with systems thinking — consistent, scalable, and aligned with business goals.
05
Validate
Usability testing, A/B testing, metrics. I close the loop by measuring impact and iterating based on real data — not assumptions.
