Project Overview

The Product

Healthcare division is a group of solutions powered by Microsoft to attend and provide solutions to health industries. It helps manage bookings and automate the processes of communication with medicine providers and MRI labs.

The Goal

Create an engaging app and portal for Telemedicine, using Microsoft cutting edge technology to connect patients with specialists and physicians seamlessly.

The Problem

Due to the pandemic, US Healthcare and Insurance markets created useful but hard to understand and non-automated portals. Specialists can't save vital patient information and all arrangements are done by phone or filling large forms.

Competitors Analysis

Feature Johns Hopkins MyChart Teladoc
Medical Care Request Uses chatbot to screen non-severe problems. Books using chatbot, fully automated. Recognizes previous visits and suggests specialists. Doctors can provide attention from their homes. Virtual waiting room. Manage bookings and send notifications.
Virtual Visit Patient can choose any clinic (virtual or not) if their insurance company allows it. Can provide non-medical treatment via chatbot. Can open virtual rooms and medical records from everywhere. First line of attention provided by a physician.
Pharmacy Patient chooses best pharmacy. Clinical record also has information about previous medicine providers. Prescriptions are sent to patient's email directly.

Design

Prototyping & Final Design

After validating user flows with the three personas, I built high-fidelity prototypes covering the core patient journey: login → chatbot triage → appointment booking → virtual consultation. The physician portal focused on fast access to patient records and virtual room management.
